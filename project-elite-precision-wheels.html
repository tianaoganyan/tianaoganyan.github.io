eliteprecisionwheels.com

OVERVIEW

Elite Precision Wheels reupholsters and customizes steering wheels with Nappa leather, Alcantara, and custom stitching. I designed and built the site to feel as premium as the work itself, with a dark interior inspired palette, warm copper accents, and large vehicle photography. The site walks customers through materials, services, and the six step build process, then leads into a detailed quote form where they can spec their own wheel. It also explains drop off options for local customers and shipping for customers nationwide.
