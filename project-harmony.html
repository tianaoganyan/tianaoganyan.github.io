HARMONY

A prototype concept for a music-based social platform where AI recommends songs based on listening behavior, then connects users through shared taste.

OVERVIEW

Harmony is a prototype concept for a social music discovery platform created as a web design and coding project. The primary focus of this work was the UI/UX design, visual identity, and front end development of the website. Rather than building a fully functional platform, the project explores how the interface and user experience could work through a designed and coded website prototype. The concept imagines a platform where users receive AI driven music recommendations based on listening behavior and can connect with others who share similar taste, with features such as music discovery, following and engagement, and playlist generation that can be random or genre based.
